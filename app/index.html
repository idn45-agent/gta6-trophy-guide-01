    Roadmap to the platinum

    Four stages, built from the official descriptions: play the story, finish the stranger strands, clear the activities and collectibles, then sweep the last counters. Each stage shows its own progress.

    How the stages were built

    1. Stage 1 — Story. Chapters 1–4 and the named story missions. Every trophy here is a story or mission trophy and cannot be missed.
    2. Stage 2 — Strangers & side missions. The character chains (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), the 8 stranger strands, Bret's hunt and the two partner trophies. Do the partner trophies while both characters are available.
    3. Stage 3 — Activities & collectibles. Shooting ranges, drone races, races, stunts, WAiNK scans, cards, meditation spots, vaults, vehicles and money. This is the long middle of the run — work it region by region.
    4. Stage 4 — Platinum cleanup. 100% Completion and the platinum itself. Open the 100% checklist in the Progress menu and let it steer the final sweep.

    Missable trophies

    Only a few trophies can be missed or made much harder by playing past them. Everything else stays available in free roam after the story. Do these on your first playthrough, in the order shown.

      Next-Up Planner

      The most sensible next trophies, picked from what you have already earned: story first, then the quick wins in your current stage, then the longest grinds last.
