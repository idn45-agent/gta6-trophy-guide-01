    ⚠ The one missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. The first meditation can only be done in Chapter 1, at Jason’s house: after the main mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. It becomes permanently unavailable after Chapter 1, and it is still unconfirmed whether it is required for the trophy — do it to be safe. Everything else can be earned after the story.

    Also: cheats disable trophies and saving, there is no mission replay, and there are 15 manual save slots — keep a save at the start and end of each chapter.

    The road to the platinum

    1. Step 1 — Finish the story 60–80 hours

      Play through all 5 chapters. Story trophies pop as you go: 4 chapter trophies, the final-mission gold, 6 mission trophies, and the 5 side-character finales (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone). Do the Chapter 1 meditation above, scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study new animals through the binoculars as you meet them — that progress already counts toward the 100% checklist even though you cannot see it yet.

      Earns: 12 story trophies + 5 side-character finales + the final-mission gold.

    2. Step 2 — The 100% Completion checklist ~100 hours

      After the story, the checklist appears under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion: most side quests are not required, collectibles are only partly required, and you need any 70 of the 80 challenges. Full-requirement items: all robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests. On the way you will finish: 250 stunts (300 for the checklist), 42 shooting-range golds, 14 drone-race locations, 20 race wins, 3 card sets, 100 WAiNK scans, 100 vehicles, 5 bank vaults, one illegal operation per gang, and the 100% gold itself.

      Earns: A Long Road (gold) and most of the activity, collectible and robbery trophies.

    3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and the leftovers ~10 hours

      Strangers & Freaks are not needed for 100%, so finish the 8 counting strands last (Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete are confirmed to count). Then sweep up whatever is left: the 4 trophies with no verified method yet, the combat one-offs, and the money trophies ($250,000 laundered, $100,000 spent, all 8 garages).

      Earns: Strangers & Freaks (silver) and the remaining miscellaneous trophies — then the platinum.

      How this guide is sourced

      Names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them (as listed by Exophase and cross-checked against PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements). Each guide is written in this guide’s own words from the cited sources — mainly PowerPyx’s trophy guide and roadmap — and never copied from them. Four trophies have no verified step-by-step method yet (In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin): their guides say so honestly instead of inventing steps, and point at what the official description itself implies.
